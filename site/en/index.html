Side A = PT (president) or the leading mayoral candidate in the municipality. "Above the threshold" = an absolute difference of 5 percentage points or more that also exceeds the normal variation between rooms of the same building (|z| ≥ 2). Another flagged sections have no comparable neighbour and are compared with their municipality, separately (see the technical document).

A flag means the section reached one of the operational thresholds defined in the study. It does not reveal the choice of anyone who voted late or was enabled manually.

Manual enablements 2026 R1: voters enabled without biometrics in the 2026 first round, summed over all sections of the building; in brackets, how many and what share were authorised by a fingerprint that the voting machine does not associate with a registered poll worker. Reference: 39% in a random sample of 5,000 sections. Values above that usually mean a poll worker whose fingerprint was not registered at opening: a traceability gap, not an improper vote. Computed for high-priority polling places; the field kit (PDF, in Portuguese) lists the sections to check and the observation steps for election day.

03 / HOW TO READ

An observation.
Not an accusation.

The study combines operational records with aggregated results per polling section. The aim is to raise verifiable questions and support further observation. The score of each polling place is an order of priority for observation, not a probability of irregularity.

Read the technical document (Portuguese) ↗
01

Public sources

Urn tally reports (BU), voting-machine logs, machine replacement records and voter-profile data, from the TSE Open Data Portal.

02

Explicit references

The main result compares each section with its neighbours in the same building. Sections without a comparable neighbour are compared with their municipality and kept separate, because the base rate there is different.

03

Limits in plain sight

Queues, biometrics, infrastructure and the make-up of the electorate can explain the differences. Association does not establish cause. How late voters voted is unknown.

The analysis doesn't

Organised datasets to query, reproduce and propose improvements without repeating the whole collection.
