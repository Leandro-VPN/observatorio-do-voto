03 / HOW TO READ

An observation.
Not an accusation.

The study combines operational records with aggregated results per polling section. The aim is to raise verifiable questions and support further observation. The score of each polling place is an order of priority for observation, not a probability of irregularity.

Read the technical document (Portuguese) ↗
01

Public sources

Urn tally reports (BU), voting-machine logs, machine replacement records and voter-profile data, from the TSE Open Data Portal.

02

Explicit references

The main result compares each section with its neighbours in the same building. Sections without a comparable neighbour are compared with their municipality and kept separate, because the base rate there is different.

03

Limits in plain sight

Queues, biometrics, infrastructure and the make-up of the electorate can explain the differences. Association does not establish cause. How late voters voted is unknown.

Organised datasets to query, reproduce and propose improvements without repeating the whole collection.
